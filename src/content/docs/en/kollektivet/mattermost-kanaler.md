In [Mattermost](https://chat.rudbeckia.nu) there is a channel for most things. Here are the open channels currently in use — all of them are free to join, you do not need an invitation.

The links open the channel directly in the web version. If you are not logged in, you will have to log in first. Read more about how we use Mattermost on the [Digital communication](/en/kollektivet/digital-kommunikation/) page.

| Channel | Description |
| --- | --- |

## Is something missing?

If you cannot find a channel that fits, just create a new one — open Mattermost, click the **+** next to the channel list and choose **Create new channel**. Do make it open so that more people can find their way there.
