![Screenshot of Mattermost](../../../../assets/kollektivet/digital-kommunikation/mattermost.png)

Even when we are spread out across the house, Uppsala or the world, the collective is close by — we use [Mattermost](https://mattermost.com/) to keep in touch with neighbours, chat, arrange spontaneous activities and organise the cooking teams.

The board and the association also use Mattermost to discuss matters at hand. When you move in you get an invitation link — do join digitally too, so that you do not miss activities and events.

See [Mattermost channels](/en/kollektivet/mattermost-kanaler/) for an overview of the open channels currently in use, with direct links.
