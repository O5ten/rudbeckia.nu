---
title: Cleaning schedule for shared spaces
description: The monthly cleaning schedule for Rudbeckia's shared spaces.
---

Sign up for at least one slot per month. This applies to everyone aged 18 and over. Write down the date when you plan to clean and sign off when it is done. **One person = one section.** Cleaning instructions are on the noticeboard.

[📄 Download stadschema.pdf (in Swedish)](/pdfs/stadschema.pdf)

## A

- Clean the living room (vacuum the sofa once)
- The children's room
- The hobby room (the gym) and the roof room
- Polish the glass partitions indoors
- Sweep the bike room in the basement
- The rest of the basement once a month

## B

- Vacuum and wet-mop the entrance, the corridor and the lift
- Clean the toilets
- Sweep/vacuum the bike room
- Clean the work room
