On the enormous [roof terrace](../../huset/takterassen/) we currently grow mostly lettuce, chilli, tomatoes and herbs — nothing is set in stone yet. On the ground floor by the kitchen wing there is also a small berry patch with currants, gooseberries, raspberries, blackberries, strawberries and a few flowers.

If you are interested in gardening you are very welcome to join in.
