If you live in the house, you are also part of a cooking team. Every cooking team has one or more team leaders; at present there are **four** teams.

We cook every **Tuesday** and **Thursday** and dinner usually starts at 17:30.

You are expected to take part in roughly two shifts per cooking-team week. If you cannot make it at all, try to find a stand-in from another team.

## Signing up for the dinners

You sign your household up at **<https://dinner.rudbeckia.nu>**. Log in with the house
password, pick an evening and fill in how many adults and children are coming, what you
eat and any allergies. The confirmation arrives as a direct message from the house bot,
together with the evening as a calendar file.

If you eat with us most times, you can turn on a **standing sign-up** under *My
sign-ups*: fill in how you usually eat on a given weekday, and you will be counted every
time without doing anything. A sign-up for a single evening always beats the standing
one – a sign-up for zero people is exactly how you skip an evening without touching your
standing sign-up.

**Sign-up closes at 23:59 on the Friday a full week before the cooking week**, because
the cooking team shops for the whole week at once. After that you cannot sign up or
change your mind on the site – get in touch with the team leader.

**Guests** sign themselves up at <https://dinner.rudbeckia.nu/gast>, without a password.
They pick an evening and write their name and who they are visiting. The link they get
back is the receipt and the only way to change or cancel, so they need to save it.

When sign-up closes, the team leader receives the **meal list** as a direct message from
the bot: the totals the team shops by and a link to the printer-friendly list of
households and allergies.

## The cooking team's responsibilities

The cooking team sets its own budget and price per portion and is responsible for every part of the cooking:

- Deciding which recipes to cook
- Deciding who does which task
- Shopping
- Cooking
- Serving
- Cleaning the kitchen
- Cleaning the dining hall
