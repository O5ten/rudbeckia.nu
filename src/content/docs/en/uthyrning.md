Rudbeckia has more than 300 square metres of shared space, and we sometimes rent it out to other associations. It is a way of contributing to the district while bringing in a little income for the association.

## What we rent out for

The house meeting has on several occasions supported renting out to associations. What we say yes to is primarily:

- **Meetings** – above all annual general meetings and other larger meetings that an association only holds once a year or so.
- **Local events** – for example clothing swaps, flea markets and other activities that are open to the residents of Rosendal.

:::caution[We do not rent out to private individuals or companies]
We only rent out to associations. In other words, we do not rent the spaces out for private parties, corporate events or anything similar – no matter who is asking.

The reason is simple: Rudbeckia is first and foremost a **home**. The starting point is always that the house should feel safe for everyone who lives here, and that weighs heavier than any single rental.
:::

## How to go about renting

Get in touch with [the board](/en/kontakt/) at [kontakt@rudbeckia.nu](mailto:kontakt@rudbeckia.nu) and tell us:

- Which association you represent
- What kind of event it is
- When, and roughly how many of you there will be

Then we take it from there. The board considers each request individually – including whether the event can go ahead without disturbing the residents – and agrees with you on the space, the time and any compensation.

:::note[Do you live in the house?]
Then you book the shared rooms as usual via the calendar on the noticeboard and Mattermost – see [Book](/en/boka/). This page is only about renting to outside associations.
:::
