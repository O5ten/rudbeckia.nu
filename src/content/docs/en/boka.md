The shared bikes of the house are booked in the house's own booking system at
**<https://booking.rudbeckia.nu>**.

## How to book

1. Open the [booking page](https://booking.rudbeckia.nu) and log in with the house
   password. You get the password from the board or in the house Mattermost.
2. Choose what you want to book, which day, for how long and when you want to start.
3. Say who you are by searching for your name or username in Mattermost.

The confirmation arrives as a direct message from the house bot, together with the
booking as a calendar file so that you can add it to your own calendar. Under
**My bookings** you can see your upcoming bookings and cancel them.

The page is available in Swedish and English – switch language with the flag in the
top right corner.

## What you can book

| What | Where |
|------|-------|
| The electric cargo bike | The bike room at street level |
| The e-bike | The bike room at street level |

The keys and batteries hang on the shelf in the cleaning room next to the lift. Put
the bike back in the bike room at street level when you are done and put the battery
back on charge – see [The bike rooms](../huset/cykelrummet/). If something has broken,
say so in [#cykelpoolen](https://chat.rudbeckia.nu/rudbeckia/channels/cykelpoolen)
in Mattermost.

## Other things that are booked

- **Guest rooms** are booked on site via the calendar on the whiteboard by the square –
  see [The guest rooms](../huset/gastrummen/).
- **Dinners** are signed up for on the [dinner page](https://dinner.rudbeckia.nu)
  – see [Cooking teams](../kollektivet/matlagen/).
