Rosendal is a new district in southern Uppsala between Ulleråker, Kåbo and Norby, and the collective house sits in what is intended to become its centre. The district is still being built and construction noise is often noticeable, but we at Rudbeckia are fairly well spared thanks to bordering the Carlshage park and sharing a courtyard with the [Kompositören](https://bygg.uppsala.se/globalassets/uppsala-vaxer/dokument/stadsplanering--utveckling/planerade-omraden/markanvisningar/rosendal-etapp-3/kvarter-h2-balder-projektutveckling-ab) project.

## Infrastructure today

![Overview of Rosendal](../../../assets/rosendal/karta-2.png)

Rosendal already has plenty of services:

- Two grocery stores
  - A Hemköp in the northern part
  - An ICA Nära in the southern part
- [Rosendal Frukt & Grönt](https://www.facebook.com/Rosendalfrukt/) (greengrocer)
- [Rosendal Ost & Chark](https://www.facebook.com/Rosendal-OstChark-109794634880292/) (cheese and charcuterie)
- 3 pizzerias
- [Blomsterlandet](https://www.blomsterlandet.se/hitta-din-butik/uppsala-rosendal/) (garden centre)
- [UB Sushi](https://ubsushi.se/)
- [Elma All Day Dining](https://www.thefork.se/restaurang/elma-all-day-dining-r575323/meny)
- [Leijon stone oven bakery and patisserie](https://leijonbageri.se/)
- Two health centres & child health care
  - Familjeläkarna
  - Praktikertjänst
