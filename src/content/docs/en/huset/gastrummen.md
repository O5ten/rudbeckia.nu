![One of Rudbeckia's guest rooms](../../../../assets/huset/gastrummen/gastrum.png)

The guest rooms are booked on a physical calendar by the square. There are three rooms: two larger ones at street level next to the playroom and one small overnight room on floor 6 next to the lift.

## Beds

In the first guest room on the left there is a 2x90 double bed with one mattress. Above it is a loft with a 140 bed.

The second room has a bunk bed with a 140 bed at the bottom and a 90 bed on top.

## Spare keys

If the key to the guest room goes missing, spare keys can be collected from 1403 in an emergency.

## How do I book a guest room?

If you live in the house, you book on site via the calendar on the whiteboard by the square. Mark it with your name and apartment number.

## What is expected of my guest?

Your guest (or you, as the person booking) cleans the guest room and the bathroom and restores it to at least the same standard as before. There is a small cleaning trolley in the bathroom.
