The Rudbeckia collective house has 42 apartments, shared spaces on the entrance floor, a basement floor and a generous roof terrace.

## The apartment fact sheet

![Apartment plan from the fact sheet](../../../../assets/huset/bofaktablad/lagenhetsritning.png)

The fact sheet contains detailed plans of the house's apartments at a scale of **1:100**, with the corresponding apartment numbers per type. It is available as a multi-page PDF — one page per apartment type.

[📄 Download Bofaktablad.pdf (in Swedish)](/pdfs/bofaktablad.pdf)

## The entrance floor and shared spaces

![Plan of the entrance floor](../../../../assets/huset/bofaktablad/entreplan.jpg)

Most of the shared spaces are on the entrance floor: the dining hall, the commercial kitchen, the study room, the living room, the laundry room, the hobby rooms and two of the guest rooms.

## The basement floor

![Plan of the basement floor](../../../../assets/huset/bofaktablad/kallarplan.jpg)

The basement floor holds a woodworking shop with tools and bench space for your projects, a larger bike room and the apartment storage units.

## The top floor

![Plan of the top floor](../../../../assets/huset/bofaktablad/takvaning.jpg)

The top floor has a room for socialising, meditation, painting and growing plants. The roof terrace has planting boxes and plenty of room for socialising, barbecuing and late evenings.

## Site plan

![Site plan of the block](../../../../assets/huset/bofaktablad/situationsplan.jpg)

Next to Rudbeckia is a courtyard with play areas that we share with the Balder building. West of the house lies the Carlshage park.
