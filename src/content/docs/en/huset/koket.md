At Rudbeckia we are very proud of our lovely kitchen!

A fully equipped commercial kitchen with a hob, two boiling pans, a convection oven, a griddle and an industrial dishwasher. This is where the [cooking teams](../../kollektivet/matlagen/) cook every **Tuesday** and **Thursday**. The rest of the time the kitchen may be used privately — as long as you follow the scout rule and leave it at least as clean as you found it.

## How does everything in the kitchen work?

The best way to learn the kitchen is by taking part in the cooking teams.

## Can I borrow something from the kitchen?

The question has not been raised at a house meeting yet, but if you borrow equipment or ingredients you are expected to return or replace them before the kitchen needs them next.
