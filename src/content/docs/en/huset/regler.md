A loosely assembled list of rules that have grown out of need.

## General

- Avoid disturbing noise between 22.00 and 07.00, for example music with a bass line that carries into your neighbour's home.
- Turn off the lights in shared spaces when nobody is there.

## The kitchen

- No unsupervised children in the kitchen. Use the child lock on the kitchen door when needed.
- Follow the hygiene rules posted in the kitchen so that we avoid food poisoning.

## The playroom

- Parents of small children are responsible for the playroom being tidied up by the evening at the latest.

## The living room

- Do not jump on the sofa — that way it lasts longer.
- The projector must be switched off when the living room is empty.

## The laundry room

After use:

- Clean the laundry room with a cloth and a mop.
- Empty the filters in the **tumble dryer** and the **drying cabinet** after use.

## The roof terrace

After use:

- Bring in any cushions from the lounge.
- Secure loose furniture that risks blowing away.
- Bring the barbecue indoors into the roof room.

## Mattermost

Are you upset about something a neighbour said or did? Breathe, and then take it up with the neighbour directly. Digital squabbling harms the atmosphere in the house.
