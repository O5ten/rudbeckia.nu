A checklist for you as a new resident.

## Before moving in

- Read through the material on this website – especially the house [rules](/en/huset/regler/).
- Email [kontakt@rudbeckia.nu](mailto:kontakt@rudbeckia.nu) and ask for access to Mattermost if you have not received it.
- Create an account in Mattermost.
- Introduce yourself in the **entré** channel.
- Ask whether someone would like to be your mentor.
- **If you have a car:** read up on the parking options nearby.
- Get to know the bike pool and how to [book the e-bike and the electric cargo bike](/en/boka/) at
  [booking.rudbeckia.nu](https://booking.rudbeckia.nu).
- Sign up for the [shared dinners of the house](https://dinner.rudbeckia.nu) on Tuesdays
  and Thursdays – see [Cooking teams](/en/kollektivet/matlagen/).

## During the move

**A hand truck** and **two furniture sliders** in different sizes are available in the bike room on **level -1**.

:::caution[Watch out for the lift]
Be careful with the lift and never block the door as it closes.
:::
