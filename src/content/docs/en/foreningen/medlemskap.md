---
title: Membership
description: About membership in the Kollektivhuset Rudbeckia association.
---

Everyone living in the house must be a member of the association and follow its statutes. The association has two levels of membership: resident members and friend members.

## Resident members

Resident members are members of the association who live in the house. Everyone living here who has turned 18 is a resident member, regardless of the type of contract.

## Friend members

Friend members are members of the association who do not live in the house, but who have the right to take part in the house's cooking teams and activities.

## Becoming a member of the association

Email [kontakt@rudbeckia.nu](mailto:kontakt@rudbeckia.nu) and pay **SEK 300** to bankgiro **5752-5925**, marked with your name.

## All members

- Apply via [kontakt@rudbeckia.nu](mailto:kontakt@rudbeckia.nu).
- Pay the annual fee (**SEK 300** per person) to bankgiro **5752-5925** between 1 January and the last day of February to keep their membership.
- May take part in the house's dinners.
- Receive **ONE** queue point per year of membership in the association's internal housing queue.
