This page is for you who want to sublet your apartment or take in a lodger – and for the person who is going to move in.

:::caution[The most important thing first]
Rudbeckia is a collective house. **Everyone who lives in the house takes part in the shared work** – cooking teams, cleaning and house meetings – whether you hold a first-hand contract, sublet or are a lodger. Living here is not like living in an ordinary rental building. Anyone who does not want to take part in the community should not move in.
:::

## Subletting or lodging?

The difference determines whether you need the landlord's permission:

- **Subletting** is when you rent out the apartment and **do not live there yourself**. This **must be approved by Hemvist Förvaltning**, and the permission is valid for a limited period. Without their permission you put your own contract at risk.
- **Lodging** is when you **still live there yourself** and rent out a room. This does **not** require the landlord's approval.

For the house, however, the difference makes no odds. Both cases mean that a new person lives here – and then the obligations of the house apply in full.

## Everyone who lives here is a resident member

This is the decisive rule, and it is set out in § 5 of the [statutes](/en/dokument/):

> All residents who have turned 18 are considered resident members, whether or not they hold a first-hand contract. A resident member shall have the collective house as their permanent home.

So there is **no middle ground**: whoever lives in the apartment permanently is a resident member, not a guest. Under § 8, every resident member is obliged to take part in the cooking teams, in cleaning and maintaining the house, and in the house meetings.

## If you want to sublet

1. **Talk to [the board](../styrelsen/) early** – before you apply for permission or promise anything to a prospective tenant. The association needs to know who lives in the house, including lodgers.
2. **Apply for permission from Hemvist Förvaltning** if you are not staying on yourself.
3. **Let the allocation group meet the person moving in.** The group meets everyone who is on their way into the house.
4. **Tell them about the house – the whole truth.** That the community is compulsory is not fine print, it is the whole point of living here. Feel free to refer them to this page.

:::tip[Think about the queue order]
If you are not going to move back, giving notice is often better for the house than subletting: the apartment can then be offered according to the [allocation policy](../formedlingspolicy/), where resident members and friend members have priority.
:::

## If you are moving in

To move in you **must become a [member](../medlemskap/)** of the association and pay the annual fee.

You will be **assigned a [cooking team](../../kollektivet/matlagen/) and a [cleaning group](../../kollektivet/stadning/)** – taking part is compulsory. We cook together on Tuesdays and Thursdays, and you take part in roughly two shifts per cooking-team week. We clean our shared spaces ourselves; no cleaning company does it for us. Attendance at the [house meetings](../husmotet/) is compulsory.

Beyond this you are very welcome – but not obliged – to get involved in one of the house's [working groups](../../kollektivet/arbetsgrupper/).

Read the house [rules](../../huset/regler/), our [values](../vardegrund/) and the statutes, so that you know what you are moving into. Then follow the [checklist for new residents](/en/nyinflyttad/).

If you have questions this page does not answer, get in touch with [the board](../styrelsen/). The association's statutes, agreements and minutes can be found under [Documents](/en/dokument/).
