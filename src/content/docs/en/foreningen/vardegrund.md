title: Our values
description: Rudbeckia's values – how we want to live and organise ourselves together.
---

At Rudbeckia we let our values guide how we live and organise ourselves together.

### We are Rudbeckia

Rudbeckia is more than a house – it is a community. We live close to one another, share everyday life, responsibility and experiences, and we believe in the strength of cooperation and the joy of creating something together.

### We take care of the community

We see one another, help one another and build trust by sharing time, space and commitment. We look after our shared rooms and create environments where everyone feels welcome.

### We meet one another with respect

There is room for difference here. We listen, talk openly and solve problems together. We respect our shared agreements and look after both one another and the house we share.

### We choose openness over distrust

We want a house where you can be yourself, where we talk *with* one another, not *about* one another. We encourage honesty, consideration and curiosity about each other's perspectives.

### We develop Rudbeckia together

We take responsibility for the future – contributing to a sustainable everyday life, nurturing our diversity and creating new ways of living together. Together we make the house stronger, warmer and more alive with every day.
